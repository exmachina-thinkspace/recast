# Recast Demo Thesis

## One Sentence

Recast understands what a building is today, where it is heading, what it could become, and what could make that transformation economically possible.

## Two Views

### As-Is Intelligence

How well does this building work in its current form, and how resilient is that current use going forward?

As-Is is not merely a distress score. A building can be healthy today but show evidence that its current use is becoming less competitive or less resilient.

### Recast Intelligence

What is the opportunity to improve, retrofit, reposition, convert, or completely reinvent the asset?

Potential outcomes:

```text
KEEP
IMPROVE
RETROFIT
REPOSITION
CONVERT
RECAST
```

Do not turn these into arbitrary 0-100 scores yet. Use evidence-backed intelligence states first.

## What The Demo Must Prove

The demo is not a dashboard. It must show that:

1. Seattle-scale data reveals how buildings are doing and where their trajectories may be changing.
2. Recast explains the As-Is condition and trajectory of one hero building using source-backed signals.
3. The citywide view briefly shows at least one contrast building that is not dead but is worth watching.
4. The workflow enters the hero building through video, photos, plans, or walkthrough evidence.
5. Acer GN100 / VSS works as searchable building memory, not just a camera captioner.
6. Recast ranks plausible alternative futures and explains physical fit, regulatory fit, market fit, financial fit, incentive fit, risks, and unknowns.
7. The demo zooms back out to show the hero building as one example of a scalable city workflow.

## Demo Pairing

Use one primary hero and one tiny lifecycle contrast.

```text
Building A - Zombie / Distressed
As-Is: Distressed
Trajectory: Deteriorating
Recast opportunity: High
Demo role: deep dive

Building B - Still Working / Vulnerable
As-Is: currently viable signal
Trajectory: vulnerable or changing
Recast opportunity: emerging
Demo role: 5-10 second contrast in the citywide view
```

Building B should not be overexplained. Its job is to prove visually that Recast does not have to wait for failure.

## Killer Moment

The judge should remember:

```text
They see a red building,
click it,
watch Recast assemble distress signals,
ask what the building could become,
see VSS retrieve physical evidence,
and see capital-stack/incentive intelligence explain what could help make the path feasible.
```

The Acer GN100 is visible because the physical-asset understanding happens locally: walkthrough ingestion, semantic search, clip retrieval, and visual evidence extraction.


Hero building:



## What To Avoid

- Do not start directly on a preselected building unless the citywide building-intelligence reveal is already complete.
- Do not make the payoff a checklist or dispatch artifact.
- Do not treat 1700 Westlake as distressed unless the evidence supports that.
- Do not use VSS only for a lobby occupancy caption.
- Do not call limited physical evidence a whole-building correction.
- Do not claim verified incentive/funding eligibility without checking requirements.
- Do not claim whole-building feasibility from one permit package or one camera.
- Do not copy full Supabase tables to the Acer just to say the Acer is involved.
- Do not let raw private video leave the local edge environment.
- Do not hide unknowns. Unknowns are part of the credibility.
