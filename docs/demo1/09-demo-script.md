# Recast Demo Script

## Opening

```text
Recast understands what a building is today, where it is heading, what it could become, and what could make that future possible.
```

Show:

- Seattle from above;
- gray buildings where evidence is insufficient;
- a small number of highlighted attention buildings;
- two visible examples: one distressed hero, one still-working-but-vulnerable contrast.

## Step 1: Building Intelligence Layer

Narration:

```text
Recast continuously reads address-linked signals: utilization proxies, value trajectory, permits, zoning, availability, energy where available, and legal or ownership distress where we can verify it.
```

Visual:

- citywide 3D flyover;
- gray background buildings;
- attention-layer buildings;
- `Building A` distressed hero;
- `Building B` lifecycle contrast for 5-10 seconds.
- gray insufficient-evidence buildings.

## Step 2: As-Is And Trajectory

Narration:

```text
We click one building and Recast explains how it is doing now and where it appears to be heading. Not with a black-box score, but with source-backed evidence.
```

Visual:

- value/tax or availability trend;
- permit/court/ownership/energy signals where verified;
- evidence labels.

Hero default after focused recon:

```text
2601 Elliott Ave: severe office As-Is weakness, deteriorating trajectory, and a real residential-conversion Recast path to test.
```

## Step 3: Enter The Building

Narration:

```text
Records explain the trajectory. NVIDIA helps understand the physical asset itself.
```

Visual:

- walkthrough/video/photos/plans;
- Acer GN100 / VSS status;
- prompt: `Show me underutilized areas` or `Find large open floorplates`.

## Step 4: VSS Becomes Ctrl-F For The Building

Narration:

```text
VSS searches the building evidence and returns the moments that matter.
```

Visual:

- query;
- timestamped clip results;
- observed physical factors;
- limitations.

## Step 5: Alternative Futures

Narration:

```text
Now Recast asks the question that matters: what could this building become?
```

Visual:

- top 2-3 reuse scenarios;
- why the top scenario is plausible;
- risks and unknowns;
- citations from records and VSS clips.

## Step 6: Capital Stack / Incentive Intelligence

Narration:

```text
A technically plausible idea is not enough. Recast also checks what public programs, incentives, tax credits, or financing tools could help make the economics work.
```

Visual:

- verified vs. potential program eligibility;
- grant/credit/loan/incentive categories;
- unknown diligence items;
- no overclaiming.

## Step 7: Zoom Back To City Scale

Narration:

```text
This distressed building is the extreme case. The same intelligence layer can also help owners future-proof buildings before they become distressed.
```

Visual:

- city flyover returns;
- hero building remains highlighted;
- other candidates remain visible.

## Closing

```text
We used a zombie building because it is the extreme case. But Recast does not have to wait for a building to fail. Every building has a present and a possible future. Recast understands both, and finds the path between them.
```
