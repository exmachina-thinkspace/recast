# Recast 60-Second Pitch

## Intent

Make the building itself the protagonist: team credibility, the scale of the commercial real estate problem, Recast as the intelligence layer, NVIDIA VSS/Acer GB100 as the physical-understanding engine, and the payoff of turning stranded buildings into second-life opportunities.

## Script

We are entrepreneurs and real estate operators with 30 years of experience, and we have been staring at the commercial real estate vacancy problem for decades.

Today, we are facing the worst commercial real estate environment of our lifetime. Recast turns that crisis into opportunity.

Using NVIDIA VSS and the Acer GB100, Recast combines financial distress signals, building data, city data, floor plans, and live video to understand a building and ask one question:

What could this building become?

Walk through a property with an iPhone, and Recast can see the physical space, understand the economics, and evaluate alternative uses, from multifamily and assisted living to data centers and coworking.

Then it gives the building a Recast Score and shows exactly what could increase that score, including incentives and grants that could make the conversion viable.

We are not just identifying distressed buildings.

We are giving buildings a second life.

## Landing Line

We are giving buildings a second life.
